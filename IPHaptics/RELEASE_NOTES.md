**IPHaptics** is TPHaptics for iPhone, built on Apple's public Core Haptics framework.

This is an **unsigned IPA**. iOS won't run it directly: sign and install it with a sideloading tool that uses
your own Apple ID or certificate (AltStore, Sideloadly, SideStore, or TrollStore on supported versions).

Needs an iPhone 8 or later on iOS 16+. Haptics only play while the app is open on screen.

Highlights: eleven patterns including smooth swells and rumbles, a 16-step sequencer, a Paint screen where the
height of a line you draw becomes vibration strength, a continuous hum you can reshape live, a feel pad, Morse,
a breathing guide, microphone-to-haptics (bass, mids, highs or loudness), and three games.

Not tested on a physical iPhone before release: it was built and checked by CI only. Please report anything that
doesn't feel right at https://github.com/jgm240/TPHaptics/issues.
